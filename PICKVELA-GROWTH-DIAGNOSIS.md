## Bottlenecks in order

1. **Reach to action:** The owner's Account Check screenshot says no unresolved issues, but that tool does not establish the first video's For You eligibility. The public page still reports zero plays, and we have no owner-side reach or retention figures. The personal profile currently has no website field, so viewers cannot tap straight to the poll. A typed `buylink-ng.onrender.com` end-card address is hard to remember and still carries the former name. The next candidate asks for a useful A/B answer directly in TikTok comments.
2. **Brand trust:** The WhatsApp Channel preview says BuyLink NG wholesale while the video/site say PickVela consumer choices. Until renamed, it should not be the main destination from a video.
3. **Reason to pass it on:** A generic “vote and share” request provides little social payoff. The post-vote page now states the user's own A/B choice and asks whether a friend would choose the same. There is no reward, fake popularity, or invented referral identity.
4. **Seed audience:** A WhatsApp Channel broadcasts to existing followers; it does not create the first audience on its own. The owner explicitly ruled out recruiting personal WhatsApp contacts because they are not targeted. Public TikTok content is the primary discovery path, with relevant public communities used only where their rules permit. Never count a browser token as a verified Nigerian person.
5. **Measurement:** Current event log counts poll-page opens and share-button taps, not TikTok video impressions, homepage visits, completed forwards, WhatsApp reads, or verified people. The present data cannot support a viral coefficient claim. Keep owner-side TikTok figures, consenting outreach counts, poll votes and share clicks in separate columns.
