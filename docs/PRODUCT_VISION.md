# Product Vision

Africa Buyer AI gives Chinese exporters an evidence-based way to find and assess African B2B buyers without treating scraped names as sales-ready leads.

The initial customer is a Chinese small-appliance supplier evaluating Nigerian importers and wholesalers. The first paid outcome is a package of 20 A/B-grade companies. Every delivered company must include a lawful, usable company contact, source evidence, and a reason it matches the supplier.

The long-term product is an agent-assisted research and follow-up workspace. Automation may discover, normalize, verify, score, and draft. A human must approve every external contact.

Commercial readiness is earned through S0 evidence. Until every frozen metric is measured and passes, the product state remains `undetermined` and the UI must not imply otherwise.
