# Product Requirements

## S0 user outcome

An analyst can review candidate buyers, their evidence, verified company contacts, assessment grade, duplicate status, provider authorization, and acquisition cost. A reviewer can see why a record is or is not eligible for the 20-company delivery.

## Initial views

1. Overview: current workflow and explicit measurement gaps.
2. Buyers: filterable candidate-company review list.
3. Buyer detail: identity, match rationale, contacts, and evidence.
4. Evidence: source claims and review status.
5. Contacts: lawful-use and verification status.
6. Costs: actual spend and qualified-buyer unit cost.
7. S0 acceptance: nine frozen metrics with numerator, denominator, threshold, and exclusions.

## Frozen acceptance rules

- 100 real, unique candidate companies.
- At least 90% verified operating businesses.
- At least 70% relevant A/B-grade importers or wholesalers.
- At least 15 confirmed grade-A importers.
- At least 60 of 100 with verified lawful usable company contacts.
- At most CNY 8 data cost per qualified A/B buyer, excluding labor.
- All 20 delivery companies are A/B and meet the contact requirement.
- Three Chinese suppliers each assess 10 records; acceptance is at least 70%.
- At least one core provider has written commercial SaaS authorization.

Any unmeasured requirement keeps overall S0 `undetermined`. Demo data is excluded.

## Out of scope for this foundation

Real buyer procurement, paid provider integrations, automated messages, production publication, and live database migration execution.
