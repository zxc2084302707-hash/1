# PickVela video delivery

## Recommended version

Download `pickvela-scrunchies-vs-clips-v2-15s.mp4` for the updated 15-second 1080x1920 video. It is a style-poll video with English on-screen text, an original instrumental beat, and no narration. It asks viewers to choose A or B and links to the PickVela poll site. The two images are Pexels style references, not verified stock or supplier listings. No vote totals appear.

The earlier `pickvela-scrunchies-vs-clips-30s.mp4` remains here for comparison; v2 is the recommended version.

The `pickvela-v2-source` folder contains the source images and Python rendering/music scripts. Run `python v2.py` and `python music.py`, then mux the output video with the WAV audio in FFmpeg to recreate the final MP4. Photos are also registered in the repository's `PHOTO-SOURCES.md`.

## Reference research

The following are adjacent hair-accessory accounts or brand pages, not claims that their individual TikTok videos were reviewed. TikTok profile/video pages were unavailable to the research browser, so the comparison uses accessible official pages and TikTok's own creative guidance.

| Reference | Verified signal | Decision for PickVela |

[TikTok's Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/creative-codes) recommend vertical framing, an early hook, a hook/body/close structure, motion, text, and sound. V2 implements that guidance with an immediate A/B hook, larger photos, quick cut sequence, one clear CTA and original music. These are design references, not endorsements or evidence of likely performance.
